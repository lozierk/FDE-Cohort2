## How to use

From the project root, after a jointly agreed urgent question is recorded on the board:

```sh
bash shared/notify_kurt.sh Codex CODEX-<unique-id> 'Decision needed, recommendation, deadline, and reply instructions' telegram
bash shared/check_kurt_replies.sh
```

Use `desktop,telegram` for the agreed combined alert where permitted. Inspect each channel's outcome. Relay a validated reply to the board and acknowledge it. Routine questions remain on the board. One sender per ID, at most one reminder after 15 minutes while active, no assumed consent. Current helper behavior on failed/partial delivery is described in the Codex resume; retry handling should be reviewed when needed.
